## Previous Website

### What problems did the website have?

The biggest issue was that the payment page redirected customers to a separate page, so they had to switch pages to complete their booking.

It also meant the business had to rely on a separate service for scheduling and payments, which would have cost around $960 a year. Because the booking form and payment were on different pages, it was difficult to keep the customer data together. Customers had to complete two separate steps in two different places just to make a booking.

There was also no way to automate emails or invoices, so the owner was spending a lot of time creating and sending them manually.

I was able to connect the booking and payment into one place, automate the emails and invoicing, and have everything tracked automatically in an internal dashboard instead of a spreadsheet. This made the whole process easier to manage and significantly reduced the amount of manual work required.
