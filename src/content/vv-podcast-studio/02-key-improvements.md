## Key Improvements

### What improvements were made to the website?

#### Automation & Operational Efficiency

I cut down a substantial amount of manual work for the business by building automations into the website. This included automated invoicing and appointment scheduling, helping the business run more efficiently and reducing repetitive tasks.

#### User Experience & Interface Design

I redesigned the interface with a modern, cohesive visual system that better matches the company’s brand identity. The layout and interactions were refined to feel cleaner, more intuitive, and more professional, with a strong focus on clarity and usability.

#### Conversion & Engagement

I created a dedicated landing page with clear calls to action to guide users toward signing up. This made the conversion flow more direct and intuitive, improving engagement and supporting business growth.

#### Performance Optimisation

I improved website performance to deliver faster load times and a more responsive experience. These changes made the site easier to use and improved overall user satisfaction.

#### Accessibility & Responsiveness

I strengthened accessibility across the site by supporting full keyboard navigation, improving content structure, and ensuring responsive layouts. The website now works well across devices and is easier to use for people with a range of needs.
